The First Internet Backgammon Server · Est. 1992

Roll, double, bear off. A single-player parlor and a living window onto FIBS — the same felt the world has played on since 1992, redrawn with a clean modern hand. Free to play, right in the browser.

  • Play a bot on FIBS — log in to the real server, browse who is online, invite a bot, or watch a match.
  • Play against the computer — eight settings: Harry Heuristic for an offline warm-up, or Gary Gammon — GNU Backgammon calibrated from novice to world-class.
  • Local two-player — one screen, one board, pass the device between turns.
