fibscli · design exploration

Four ways to
reimagine the board.

Same backgammon app — local play, Gary Gammon AI, and the live FIBS client — rendered in four committed aesthetic directions. Open each, walk Home → Board → FIBS, and pick the world this app should live in.

↗
01 — warm · tactile · light

Analog Luxe

Walnut, brass, and wool felt. Hand-turned checkers with real weight. A private backgammon club rendered in a browser.

 ↗
02 — nostalgic · monospace · dark

Retro Terminal

The name made literal. Amber phosphor, scanlines, and a blinking cursor — a 1990s FIBS client that never stopped glowing.

 ↗
03 — confident · typographic · light

Modern Editorial

Ivory, ink, and one sharp vermillion. Gallery whitespace and a crisp geometric board — the design-forward answer to chess.com.

 ↗
04 — high-energy · glass · dark

Neon Arcade

Dark glass, cyan-vs-magenta glow, and dice that want to tumble. Backgammon as a dopamine-forward esport.
Each file is self-contained — open it directly in a browser, no build step. Use the in-page nav to move between Home, Board, and FIBS. These are throwaway visual mockups to choose a direction; none are wired to game logic.
